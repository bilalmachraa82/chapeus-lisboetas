== Description ==

We built AIWU to help site owners automate content creation and customer interaction — with tools more flexible than ChatGPT or any single AI engine alone.
The plugin seamlessly connects modern AI to your WordPress site, letting you write, optimize, and engage visitors without ever leaving your dashboard.

From generating content in bulk and improving existing posts to chatting with users, managing products, and adding internal links — everything happens natively inside WordPress, with full control over tone, prompts, and language models.

AIWU supports multiple providers, including OpenAI, Gemini by Google, Claude AI (via MCP) and DeepSeek. You bring your own API key and decide how each tool fits your workflow.

Paste plugin folder into here

B) DIRECT UPLOAD VIA ADMIN AREA

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= What is AIWU and how is it different from other AI plugins? =
AIWU is an all-in-one AI automation plugin for WordPress. It combines content generation, chatbot support, WooCommerce editing, and internal linking — all integrated natively into your site. Unlike basic prompt tools, AIWU gives you full control over tone, structure, fields, and automation.

= Do I need my own OpenAI or Google API key? =
Yes. AIWU does not charge extra for usage — you connect your own key (OpenAI, Gemini, Claude A or DeepSeek) and pay the provider directly.

= What does the chatbot do? =
AIWU includes a customizable live chat assistant that can answer questions, recommend products, guide users, and collect leads — all using your content and settings.

= Can it replace my live chat tool? =
In most cases — yes. The assistant handles up to 80–90% of conversations automatically, with optional fallback to human support when needed.

= Can I train the chatbot on my content? =
Yes. You can upload your own datasets, add structured instructions, or generate embeddings (RAG) based on your site. The assistant then uses that data to reply in your brand’s voice.

= Does AIWU support WooCommerce? =
Yes. You can bulk-generate or update product content — including titles, descriptions, images, tags, reviews, and more — directly in the WooCommerce editor.

= Can I fine-tune my own model? =
Yes. AIWU supports OpenAI fine-tuning and custom embeddings, so you can personalize generation based on your own examples.

= How does RSS-based automation work? =
Connect any feed, and AIWU will turn each item into a structured article. You can apply prompts, control formatting, and even auto-generate matching social media posts.

= Does the chatbot support human fallback? =
Yes. You can let users request human help, set alert notifications, and define how and when handover happens.

= Will it work with my theme or builder? =
Yes. AIWU works out of the box with all major themes and builders. Use shortcodes, widgets, or floating buttons to place AI features anywhere you need.

= What is Claude MCP Integration and how does it work? =
Claude MCP Integration allows Claude AI to directly control your WordPress site through conversation. Using the Model Context Protocol, Claude can create pages, manage content, upload images, and handle all website operations by simply chatting with you. Just say "Create a landing page" and Claude builds it automatically — no coding or technical skills required.
